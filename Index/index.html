Four principles behind results

Progressive overload

Increase weight, repetitions or sets gradually. Muscle adapts only to rising demand.

Recovery

Allow about 48 hours before training the same muscle group hard again.

Protein

1.6 to 2.2 g per kg of body weight daily supports muscle repair and growth.

Sleep

7 to 9 hours per night supports recovery, performance and appetite control.
